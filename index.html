Inclusive opportunities for individuals and families to learn, grow, engage, and heal.

At Heart of the City Development Center, we believe everyone deserves a place to belong. Through inclusive education, advocacy, life-skills development, and family-centered support, we empower children and adults of all abilities to reach their full potential.

“The most beautiful things in the world…must be felt with the heart”

Our Daily Mission Is Built on Four Core Values.

Our Key Focus

Learn

We provide a safe, welcoming, and inclusive environment where children and adults of all abilities are empowered to learn, grow, and thrive.

Learn More

Grow

Our enrollees will continue to develop and gain daily living skills that are critical to helping them become productive members of society.

Contact Us

Engage

Social skills are an important skill that can help individuals succeed in all aspects of life. Developing these skills allows our enrollees to make positive contributions to society.

Learn More

Heal

We provide whole-family support so everyone feels included, valued, and understood. Through shared experiences and connection, we help families find hope, strength, and belonging.
